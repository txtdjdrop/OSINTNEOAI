## 6. WHAT "CONCRETE COFFINS" LIKELY ARE

Based on the data, the underground structures at the HBNC site are most likely:

1. **StormTech Chambers** - Polypropylene (not concrete) arch chambers for stormwater management under the parking lot
2. **Concrete Utility Vaults** - For electrical transformers (SCE), junction structures for storm drains
3. **Concrete Catch Basins** - Storm drain inlet structures (Standard 301-303)
4. **Junction Structures** - Concrete structures connecting storm drain pipes (Standard 304-307)
5. **Underground Electrical Vaults** - Concrete vaults housing 220V transformers per SCE General Order #128

## Summary

The HBNC (Huntington Beach Navigation Center) at 17642 Beach Blvd / 17631 Cameron Lane is a 174-bed homeless shelter operated by Mercy House since December 2020. The site sits in **Tract 405** on **active subsidence** land with loamy sand soil. The City invested ~$8.2M+ in acquisition and construction, with Orange County covering on-site utility and shelter installation costs.

The "concrete coffins" are likely a combination of:
- StormTech underground stormwater chambers (polypropylene)
- Concrete utility vaults for electrical transformers (220V)
- Concrete storm drain junction structures
- Concrete catch basins

The site has active CIP projects for channel repairs (soil surcharge issues) and water main replacement, confirming the underground infrastructure problems.
