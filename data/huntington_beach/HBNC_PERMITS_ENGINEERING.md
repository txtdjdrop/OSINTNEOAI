## UNDERGROUND INFRASTRUCTURE AT HBNC

### StormTech Underground Chambers
- **Product:** Polypropylene arch chambers (NOT concrete)
- **Manufacturer:** Advanced Drainage Systems (ADS)
- **Models:** SC-310, SC-740, MC-3500, MC-4500, MC-7200, DC-780
- **Design Life:** 75 years
- **Use:** Underground stormwater detention/infiltration under parking lots
- **Installation:** Under parking lots, roads, playgrounds

### SCE Underground Electrical Vaults
- **Type:** Concrete boxes housing transformers and circuit breakers
- **Coverage:** 20,000+ vaults in SCE service area
- **Repair Process:** Composites for Infrastructure (fiberglass + liquid compound)
- **Extended Life:** 50 years
- **Recent Local Work:** Balboa Peninsula, Newport Beach

### Storm Drain System
- **Total Network:** 218 miles
- **City-Owned:** 120 miles
- **Design Standard:** 10-year storm (85% capture), 100-year storm (85% capture)
- **Detention:** Huntington Lake (34 acres), Talbert Lake (95 acres)

### Utility Conduits (HB Standard 215)
- Standard 215/1-3 of 3: Utility conduit locations in r/w
- Standard 220: Micro-trenching (2022)
- Standard 217: Parkway Repair for Utility Box Installation

## Summary

The HBNC at 17642 Beach Blvd was designed by **RPM Team**, graded by **TTS Engineering** ($670K), and environmentally overseen by **EEC Environmental**. The site contains:

- **Underground stormwater chambers** (StormTech polypropylene)
- **SCE electrical vaults** (concrete, 220V transformers)

- **Utility conduits** (per HB Standard 215)

The building permits are available on the Accela portal but require manual search. The actual construction plans and as-built drawings are likely held by:
- Huntington Beach Public Works Engineering
